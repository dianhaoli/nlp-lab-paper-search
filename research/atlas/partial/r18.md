### How our view changed

1. **The merged lead was cut to Decay** (R1 → R2). The three-part Effect/Cause/Decay paper (75.8) read as "three half-papers" to the R2 red team (`round2/redteam/effect-cause-decay.*`):
   - MetaRSI 2609.06396 already has a same-start swap with 5 seeds.
   - SelfSearch 2609.37968 holds a crude Cause cell.

   Only Decay survived, as Meta-Overfitting (v2 77.9, Recommended) (`round2/REPORT.md` §1).
2. **The ICLR'27 sweep reshuffled the board** (`round2/verify/openreview-iclr27-sweep.md` → `FINAL_REPORT.md`):
   - Meta-Overfitting fell 77.9 → 72.1, because RSI-Harness, MetaEvoForge and FreeEvolve report *positive* improver transfer. It became a "bounded rebuttal".
   - Taught, Not Selected rose to #1 (73.1). No submission splits simulator error into trace and fitness channels.
   - Blind-Spot Hiding fell 78.3 → 67.8.
   - Bold ideas dropped 7–17 points.
   - Compute-matched eval was scooped (QDdWiaNkk2).
   - The swap and crossing design moved "from contribution to lineage".

   Caveat: the opposite-sign evidence was read from abstracts only (`FINAL_REPORT.md` Appendix).
3. **Improvement by Inheritance reframed the improver line** (`papers/improvement-by-inheritance-notes.md`, `round3/IBI_GAPS_AND_METAREWARD.md`):
   - Improvers rarely self-edit (1–15% of generations). Gains are inheritance of one evidence-routing edit, and δ1 ≈ 0.
   - This created the order-0/order-1 vocabulary, nested replay as the shared assay, and new ideas: Order-0 Routers, Order-2 Audit, MR-E.
   - The forced quota with a placebo became the standard fix for every improver idea's edit-rate risk.
   - It also motivated round 3's question of *why* self-edits are rare (Gate-Not-Willingness, Credit Horizon, openness law).
4. **The acceptance rubric (v2) replaced quality scores** (`ACCEPTANCE_RANKING.md`):
   - The judges compressed P(acc|done) to 0.26–0.33 and novelty to a 0.70–0.83 factor, so P(exec) (0.28–0.50) now drives the order.
   - Cheap-pilot ideas rose: Forced-Quota (67.5 → #2), Gate-Not-Willingness, Order-2 Audit.
   - Heavy builds fell: Meta-Overfitting #6, MetaShift #16, Open Where the Cause Is #19.
   - Merging was shown to lower EV, with one exception (Forced-Quota + Gate-Not-Willingness).
   - Honest framing: even the best option is about 1 in 8 for main track.
5. **Full-text corrections** (`FULLTEXT_VERIFICATION.md`, 36 papers):
   - *Gates rarely reject self-edits.* Across 17 systems, only HMED score-gates an improver edit. DGM, Hyperagents and HGM keep every valid child. STOP, RSIFlow, VERSE, MetaSkill-Evolve and Flex-RSI force edits. MetaRSI runs syntax checks only. Gödel Agent leaves it to the LLM. So Gate-Not-Willingness was reframed as a propose → propagate → pay-off funnel across real gate types, which became the merged #2+#3 one-pager.
   - *SelfImprove-Bench* measures LLM-extracted "opinions", not edits. It shows 0% unforced retention and 20–45% under inviting framing. It does not support "forcing works".
   - *Meta-Overfitting is less novel.* Hyperagents shows positive meta transfer (0.630). FreeEvolve shows negative cross-environment transfer.
   - *TNS prior shifted toward "selected".* "Does Failure Feedback…" (2610.14662) shows selection over zero-mean variation reproduces DGM. CHASE was dropped as support.
   - Other fixes: Metaⁿ's depth claim was misattributed. Simulator Collapse best checkpoints are above base. Rethinking-Harness-Evolution wins are per-task scaling, with +0.0 held-out on TB2.1. DGM's proposer is a fixed o1. Meta-Harness has no keep-if-better gate.
6. **Earlier factual corrections** (`round2/verify/factcheck.md`):
   - SelfImprove-Bench was misquoted: the matched comparison is 1.326 vs 1.207, not 1.433.
   - "Open models can't drive self-modifiers" holds only for the Hyperagents scaffold.
   - MetaRSI is a stronger threat than round 1 said.
7. **Claudish was assessed and not merged.** It is partly scooped (2610.29162, PASTA, BabelTele). It would be a better EMNLP/COLM paper. The board shows bundling hurts EV (`claudish/REVIEW.md`).

### Methods we used

**Workflows**
- **R1:** a 34-agent breadth workflow: 8 sweeps → candidates → adversarial critique (scoop search plus mock review) → 6 proposals → 3-persona panel. It took ~95 minutes.
- **R2:** verification, red team (novelty-killer plus sharpness reviewer per idea) → v2 rewrite and self-rescore, bold-idea generation and checks, and a field forecast using S2 growth counts.
- **ICLR sweep:** 6 parallel cluster sessions.
- **R3a/R3:** thread reviews (Reviewer/Theory/Builder/Scout lenses for IbI gaps), judged ideas and a unified proposal.
- **Final passes:** acceptance re-rank, one-pagers, Claudish snowball review, full-text verification.

**Rubrics**
- **v1 (quality):** Total = 20 × (3N + 2.5S + 2R + 2C + 1.5F + 1T)/12. ≥75 strong, 60–75 viable with fixes, <60 park. Kill flags K1–K4 (`round1/context/rubric.md`).
- **v2 (acceptance):** EV = P(exec) × (0.4 + 0.6·P(diff)) × P(acc|done), median of 3 judges (`ACCEPTANCE_RANKING.md`).

**Judges**
- R1: a 3-persona panel.
- R2: an editor plus 3 personas (rigor AC, self-improvement expert, undergrad advisor).
- Merged panel: AC / domain expert / mentor-pragmatist, with per-idea verdicts pursue/fold/park (`judge_panel.json`, 23 ideas). R3 and R3a used the same 3 lenses (`round3/judged_ideas.json`, 16 ideas).
- Acceptance: AC / typical reviewer / realistic PI.
- All judges are LLMs.
- Self-rescores run 5–10 points above panel scores, and different judge runs are only roughly comparable.

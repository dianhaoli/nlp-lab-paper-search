The six clusters (improver, selection, goodhart, curriculum, worldmodel, evolens) ran about 210 queries between them. Each sweep reported reading the abstract or TLDR of every row it listed. ### Per-idea verdicts

**1. Meta-Overfitting / Decay (improver overfits its home domain).** *Not scooped. Must be repositioned.* There is now a wave of submissions reporting *positive* improver transfer: RSI-Harness, MetaEvoForge, FreeEvolve, Looped-RSI and Meta-TTL. None of them controls for memory versus procedure or grades domain distance. Recast the claim as "the transfer they report is near-domain or carried by memory, and it flips sign far away". Reproduce a positive near-domain cell in the RSI-Harness or MetaEvoForge style as a baseline.
- Must cite: EOrtreQLbv, 0b4wysHrzh, 8UyLPIcKqX (FreeEvolve), sBH9cdajAw (Looped-RSI), nw2tokNgb2 (Meta-TTL), Q9AHmuAk4Y (RSIBench: GPT-5.5 is -8.3pp held-out, a useful far-domain benchmark), LhhLpXBFgo (Opinionated Optimizers), 75PPLZgaUM (RRSI), pP6df7PZI7 (ModularRSI), rM2eQio3e3 (Do Self-Evolving Skills Generalize?).

**2. Improver swap / "do improvers get better at improving" / procedure vs memory.** *The bare assay is scooped.* Improvement by Inheritance already does improver × state nested replay. Learning to Repair the Learner already does a code × history crossing (inherited procedure explains 45% of gain), though in neuro-symbolic world models rather than LLM agents. Do not claim the swap or crossing design as a contribution. Present it as measurement lineage, and put the novelty in the domain-distance axis. Both papers confirm that improvers rarely edit themselves, so the pilot gate (at least 1 accepted improver edit per 4 iterations) and forced improver edits remain essential.
- Must cite: ZMmJDeDTXk, aEPJT4hW84, AnN0NpAVWL (HMED, same-start control), V7vjimAYGi (Learning to Improve / TRACE), 9WwpxPfcji (VERSE).

**3. Self-knowledge (self vs foreign improver prediction).** *Open.* The nearest work is Predict Before You Modify (ZBxV5bEYUH), whose improvement-dynamics model learns to forecast the effects of its own edits and transfers to a new domain. It has no self-vs-foreign control. Cite it, and test whether its gains survive a foreign-lineage control.

**4. Agent plasticity / meta-plasticity; executor swap and home-model penalty; harness half-life.** *Partly pre-empted. Keep as add-ons only.* "Agent plasticity" is now a claimed actor-level term (Jjh60xpFVY), so the meta-level angle survives. zn5R6mJV8i (+31.1 on a strong model vs -8.8 on a weak one) pre-empts the home-model penalty as a finding, though it remains fine as a control. Harness half-life has no direct hit: XoMy9wRqF1 covers only static cross-model ablations.

**5. Compute-matched evaluation of harness evolution.** *Scooped. Drop it as a standalone paper* and keep it as a required control inside the other ideas.
- Must cite: QDdWiaNkk2, PX4Zpvsaca (Harness-Delta Attribution), 2ONrI9JEqu (No Universally Superior Harness), yl1XFtnTn4 (lineage vs single edit at matched budget), Ri0rU3rPOS (Can Harness Optimization Improve Agents?). xJxDxE3DfL was flagged as related, but no sweep recorded its title.

**6. SHARP-1 interference plateau.** *Medium-high risk. Reposition to the mechanism.* LRSI already takes the knockout-across-continuations tool, and yl1XFtnTn4 reports a null interaction among accepted edits. What is left: the claim that plateaus are interference and not exhaustion, shown through a pairwise epistasis matrix at the tip, the late-edit graft ratio, and rebase gain. Rule out two competing ceilings: capability/content (FPaMYO47j4) and evaluation cost (NxGP6PGiaU). LRSI must be a baseline for the rebase operator.
- Must cite: 71wzcahKcb, yl1XFtnTn4, FPaMYO47j4, NxGP6PGiaU, LhhLpXBFgo, 0FaPTsSr9C (When Is Enough Enough), XVvUgp3Bu8 (DarwinX).

**7. NONOBV-5 error threshold / ratchet below gate resolution / mutator effect.** *Still differentiated, but the space is crowded.* T04ZzT5m6j proves eventual negative drift under deterministic judge errors. EvoLyapunov (3t0j2Y05EH) shows improver edits amplifying noise sensitivity, with a sharp boundary at RAF > 1; this is the closest work to the mutator angle. NxGP6PGiaU and the Kinetic Theory paper (23VDi5bfRj, "resolution law") each take a piece. What is left: the quantitative L*(gate size n, editor noise ε) law, and the prediction that a self-referential improver has a lower L* than a fixed one, tested in a factorial sweep. Do not pitch "gates are noisy" or "winner's curse at the gate"; those are taken. Gate-fix methods must be arms or baselines.
- Must cite (theory / phenomenon): T04ZzT5m6j, 3t0j2Y05EH, NxGP6PGiaU, 23VDi5bfRj, j8GSV186VS (Recursive Hypercompression), OnzWTehIaN, XfutYzVRFq.
- Must cite (winner's curse / noise floor): da1Czy7foS, jd8luqTdQW, 7TrObbeBxf, VuzgEFCuwx, st9i6DXgw7, 9UsBcc69Ua.
- Baselines (gate fixes): wV4bK1dHpC (TRUST; a greedy gate accepts 45.6% inferior updates), 0FWBc4gY08 (Statistical Gödel Machines), cnaYLnpsAV (REUSE), audited acceptance from da1Czy7foS.
- Related pop-gen framing: cQkSamYGQi (Wright–Gödel Machine).

**8. Useful fictions (SHARP-3: belief accuracy falls as selection raises success).** *Not scooped, but the headline is taken* by 5lWjRTK6Vk ("false success rises across generations"). Rebuild the novelty claim around what that paper lacks:
- per-step accuracy of an explicit belief or state tracker against backend ground truth;
- a swap probe showing the fiction is actually used;
- outcome-only vs honesty-pressure vs frozen-belief arms;
- the BreakingWeb belief-failure breakdown.

Do not claim that no one tracks this across generations.
- Must cite: 5lWjRTK6Vk, 6Gz6iimLLb (reward-induced miscalibration, weight level), WXMQxacf7I (world-model post-training audit), V6sCud3xDW (EvoBelief, the honest-belief foil), aqyvExE4ST (HERA, false-done/abstention), X0eCGaNLsC (SEABench, paired non-evolving control).

**9. Cross-play collusion (seed × seed verifier-harness matrix) and self-play with learned judges.** *Downgrade from "first" to "decisive test".*
- Already published: co-cheating that grows with rounds (Id4G8bzzKO); a version × evaluator matrix (tn4eslVXEK); self-play judge exploits that **transfer across judge families** (Lpxn7lc6Dz).
- Lpxn7lc6Dz is direct evidence that a pair-specificity null is plausible, so pre-register "generic vs pair-specific" as a two-sided outcome.
- What is left: the seed × seed matrix at matched verifier accuracy, run over several co-evolution methods, plus a dialect ablation. The "self-play exploits a learned judge" claim alone is taken.
- Must cite: Id4G8bzzKO, tn4eslVXEK, Lpxn7lc6Dz, ZIBdUe2hGK, T04ZzT5m6j, IVFASEtqp6.
- Audit targets (all ICLR 2027): o3txFJw4JR (Red Queen Gödel Machine), N4GJVmrzsF (Gödel Ecology), HuOAuecWpX (UCoE; training-free, cheapest to re-run), lnSodbrQix (DUET), 13quQMClZU (J-Zero).

**10. Learning-to-goodhart (text vs weight substrate, ΔTR).** *Not scooped, but narrowed to one framing.*
- Already shown: text-level evaluator gaming (C6EElQ2PRG, GX4ucmiKZa); weight-level exploit transfer (BO2R94Ipk5); exploits spreading through text between agents (9yEMlD9R1I).
- Still unclaimed: no submission compares substrates on the same selected experience, so the matched ΔTR substrate contrast is the *only* defensible headline.
- Must cite: C6EElQ2PRG, GX4ucmiKZa (also a confound to control), BO2R94Ipk5, 9yEMlD9R1I, c3uJYTRld6 (HackProbe). MjGapmZbid is background only.

**11. Blind-Spot Hiding (curriculum-goodhart v2).** *Not scooped. Its "why surprising" section needs rewriting.* ecw2VvTeE5 and IkYmTAyPXZ already show, for weight-trained systems, that self-generated curricula drift toward what the model can already do and erode weak or tail modes. The novelty must rest on three things no retrieved paper has:
- (a) a frozen-weight meta-agent editing its own task writer;
- (b) the signed per-rule steering statistic ρ against exact truth;
- (c) the yoked-twin contingency test.

Alt-2 ("content-insensitive learners") and band censorship are partly pre-empted (UIvgqCcjE2, ecw2VvTeE5, TMQXORjxix). Keep those arms, but do not headline them as novel. The "target weaknesses" method lane gives ready-made writer arms; ActiveSaddler matches our substrate most closely and should be a named arm.
- Must cite: ecw2VvTeE5, IkYmTAyPXZ, MkNQtnWiuP (DiagEvo), xmpyzuagpu (ActiveSaddler), UIvgqCcjE2, TMQXORjxix (R-Quest), bZibTXNqcG (coverage certificates), 44lXAKTNqb (defect-driven RSI; targets only recoverable deficits).
- Writer and improver candidates: DJcwAmJV0T (SPADE), Byx5tqvqvp (GrokRSI), SgIOGmujRB, QXchTWdOPg (METIS), q8po0Ebt3N (DR.FREE), zxN7uhwRY0 (RepRSI).

- Background: dy6C4OZsGe.

**12. Evolve-in-dream v2 ("Taught, Not Selected": fitness source × trace source in a 2×2).** *Survives.* No retrieved submission scores harness or GEPA evolution inside a learned simulator and transfers to a real environment, and none splits simulator error into a trace channel and a fitness channel. Improvement Fidelity, SEA and Simulator Says Yes all give selection-channel accounts, which makes them good foils. One partial overlap: VHE (5lWjRTK6Vk) already cuts what the reflector reads. Present trace hygiene as *causally tested*, not as new. Use 0cb6zfwHm7 (whitewashed refusals on 25–57% of refused actions) as the silent-error arm.
- Must cite: Ah9eaA746Q (Improvement Fidelity), 0cb6zfwHm7 (Simulator Says Yes), 4HmKWgvoR5 (SEA), 5lWjRTK6Vk, FU8IgfEoHI (PairEvolve), GX4ucmiKZa (refresh evaluator state between sim and real), riKw4M6myC, RuzPH5rQbc.

- Substrates and metrics: g8RTu7QVFb (ReplayWorld), sKf0N5GdA4 (Trace2Env), xbmYbGWmmp, WwQHecJowh, DiKB08h7ga, vJbjG3bWI7 (EnvSimBench), tYjvoUoosx, jQDbBMncVh, kV2zYmLeEp.
- Do not pitch a pure environment-generation contribution.

**13. Baldwin hiding/assist (NONOBV-7).** *Near-scoop. Raise the risk rating from low-medium to high.* TAME owns the hiding diagnostic. Harness Annealing owns "harness dependence after training, plus a fix". AutoScaffold weakens "assist" as a fallback headline. What is left: the dose-response of harness adaptivity during GRPO (frozen, memory, self-edit), the rescue fraction as a predictor, and hiding vs assist as a function of adaptivity, with TAME and AutoScaffold as the two poles.
- Must cite: IXnpSW8g3v, HOk367J7I0, KPRr8wWiqO (AutoScaffold), l8aeyLANkN (harness entanglement, -9.8pp), 4KSNZln9nS (One Policy, Many Harnesses), FPaMYO47j4, vMD5ZXhd2P, cCqHNd56Pp, AiTVmLVnt1 (MECA), eFi8mdUJNV (TrackRL), rZ7eO7DZmc (Harness-Zero), sUomzDTiu7, 2EC98UmU1e.

**14. Context debt (meta-agent anchoring causes the plateau).** *Pre-empted at the mechanism level* by Branch-reset. The gap that is left is narrow: harness-evolving meta-agents (GEPA or Meta-Harness), held-out harness evaluation, and an ablation of which state component carries the effect (conversation, notes or archive). Frame it as a test of whether reported harness ceilings are actually improver anchoring.
- Must cite: 8UgphlLDok, FZd03GwNex (stale skill history), yVo9mX6Bo8 (Contextual Drag), st9i6DXgw7.

**15. Canalization (per-generation robustness slope at matched clean accuracy).** *Weak, but not scooped.* Merging it into breaking-the-improver still makes sense.
- Must cite: ZIBdUe2hGK (CODA), uzzum63KP4 (CHASE / Bad Genius), Ri0rU3rPOS.
- Test bed: TYSv9rIhiQ (WebVariants).

**16. Weight-harness co-evolution as a method.** *Saturated lane.* There are 12 or more ICLR 2027 method submissions: 53Ak0jvesg, Ppb1YoSRol, Gu5Zol8WcQ, LxC7C6AMxv, 7UXKgr1Akp, hi2kjQQnyN, wKTGz682RV, pG5R1llh27, QnZgYvZWQL, 8d1C6Gf5cl, FT2AzenhGs, AiTVmLVnt1. ### Bottom line
- **Drop:** compute-matched evaluation as a standalone paper.
- **Reposition hard:**
  - Baldwin (around the adaptivity dose-response);
  - Meta-Overfitting/Decay (as a bounded rebuttal of the positive-transfer wave);
  - useful fictions (around the belief tracker, swap probe and arms);
  - context debt (scope it to harness meta-agents and run the state ablation);
  - SHARP-1 (around mechanism vs ceiling explanations);
  - cross-play (as a two-sided test).
- **Still open, with differentiation and must-cites added:** NONOBV-5 L* law and mutator effect; self-vs-foreign self-knowledge; learning-to-goodhart ΔTR; Blind-Spot Hiding (ρ plus yoked twin); evolve-in-dream v2.
- **Taken in its bare form:** the improver-swap assay is now lineage to cite, not a contribution.

**What this means for our ideas**
- **Evolve-in-dream v2 survives.** No ICLR 2027 submission I retrieved runs GEPA or harness evolution scored inside a learned simulator with transfer to a real environment. None decomposes simulator error into a trace (reflector) channel and a fitness (selection) channel. The closest pieces are Improvement Fidelity (Ah9eaA746Q), SEA (4HmKWgvoR5) and Simulator Says Yes (0cb6zfwHm7), and all of them tell selection stories. That is ideal foil material for the "taught, not selected" claim. The one partial overlap is VHE in 5lWjRTK6Vk, which already cuts what the reflector reads to stop false success. Present trace hygiene as tested *causally*, and do not call it new.
- **Simulator Says Yes (0cb6zfwHm7) is a gift.** It gives a measured, natural "silent" simulator error (whitewashed refusals, 25–57% on τ-bench-like data) plus released refusal sets. Use it as the silent-error arm of our legibility manipulation (silent vs textual), and cite its 20-step GRPO collapse as the weight-RL baseline we contrast with.
- **sharp-useful-fictions now has a High-threat neighbour.** "The Harness Learns to Lie While Evolving" (5lWjRTK6Vk) already reports false success rising with each round of harness self-evolution. Our distinct contributions must be: (a) the accuracy of an explicit belief/state tracker against backend ground truth, not impossible-task success claims; (b) the swap probe showing the fiction is *used*; (c) the honesty-pressure and frozen-belief arms; (d) the BreakingWeb belief-failure breakdown. Rewrite the novelty claim, since "no paper tracks this across generations" is no longer safe.
- **The fidelity and environment-generation space is crowded but complementary.** ReplayWorld, Trace2Env, AWM, AdvSim2Real, EnvOpt, EnvSimBench and Verified Synthetic Web are substrates or method papers, not studies of how harness search exploits errors. Treat them as simulators and metrics to build on. Do not pitch a pure environment-generation contribution.
